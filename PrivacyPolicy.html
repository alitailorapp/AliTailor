Ali Tailor – Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 01/08/2026 | Effective Date: 01/08/2026

At Ali Tailor, we value your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your data when you use our application.

Data Collection

When you choose to sign in with Google, we collect only the following information:

  • Profile Name
  • Email Address

This information is used solely for sign-in and profile display purposes. We do not access or store your Google profile picture. We do not access any other data from your Google account.

Data Usage

We use Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the app is used (such as how often it is opened and basic device information) so we can improve the app and its features. This analytics data does not include your customer records, orders, measurements, credits, or any other business data you enter — that data stays on your device. We do not use your personal data for promotional purposes.

Data Sharing

We do not share your personal data with any third parties. Your information is completely private and secure.

The following third-party services work with us:

  • Google Drive: Used for cloud backup storage
  • Google AdMob: Used for displaying advertisements

These services have their own privacy policies. We are not responsible for their data practices.

Data Deletion Policy

You can delete your data at any time:

  • Via App: You can delete individual customers, orders, credits, and collections directly from the app.
  • Via Google Drive: If you have backed up data to Google Drive, you can delete it directly from your Google Drive account.
  • Complete Deletion: Uninstalling the app will remove all locally stored data from your device.

Security

We take the following measures to protect your data:

  • Your data is stored locally on your device
  • We use Room Database for local storage
  • Google Drive backups are stored on Google's secure servers
  • We do not share your data with any third parties

Children's Privacy

This app is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Payments

All payments for subscriptions and in-app purchases are securely processed through Google Play Store. We do not store any payment information on our servers.

Advertising

This app may display advertisements through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect the following information:

  • Device type (mobile/tablet)
  • Operating system version
  • Number of interactions with advertisements
  • Duration of time spent viewing advertisements

This information is used solely to improve ad experience. You can opt out of personalized ads through your device settings.

Local Data Storage

All your customer records, orders, measurements, credits, and images are stored locally on your device using a secure database. No data is uploaded to external servers unless you choose to use the Google Drive backup feature.

App Permissions

Ali Tailor requests only the following minimal permissions:

  • Camera: Used solely to capture images of cloth and designs during customer registration. These images are stored locally on your device and are never uploaded to any server. The purpose is to help you remember which fabric and design were associated with each customer.
  • Notifications: Used to send you timely reminders related to:
    • Orders that are overdue or approaching their delivery date
    • Backup reminders to help you keep your data safe
    No promotional or marketing notifications are sent.
  • Internet: Used only for:
    • Google Drive backup and restore (if you choose to enable it)
    • Displaying advertisements through Google AdMob
  • Google Account (Cloud Backup): Used only when you choose to sign in with Google for cloud backup and restore. In Android this permission is listed under the "Contacts" permission group (GET_ACCOUNTS). Ali Tailor does not read, store, or transmit your device contacts or any other account data — it only lets you choose which Google account to use for backup.

Ali Tailor does not request or use the following permissions: Storage / File Access, Location, Microphone, SMS or Phone.

All data remains on your device unless you explicitly choose to back up to Google Drive.

Data Retention

  • Your data remains on your device until you uninstall the app
  • Google Drive backups remain in your own Google account — we do not delete them
  • Sign-in information exists only while the app is active
  • Uninstalling the app automatically removes all locally stored data

Your Rights

You have the following rights:

  • Access: You can view your data at any time
  • Correction: You can correct any inaccurate data
  • Deletion: You can permanently delete your data
  • Export: You can export your data

All these rights are provided to you within the app itself.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the new policy. Important changes will be notified within the app.
